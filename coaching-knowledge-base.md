## PART 1: QUESTION LIBRARY BY TOPIC

### 🔍 Problem Reveal / Brain Dump Starters
- What are you worried about?
- What scares you right now?
- What do you wish was different?
- What isn't right?
- What is hurting?
- What are you struggling with?
- What is a problem you have?
- What is another problem you have?
- What isn't working?

### 💭 Beliefs About Yourself
- What is your opinion of you? (Write two paragraphs)
- Do you think you are living the life you are meant to live?
- What do you think about your past?
- How do you feel about what you have created in your life so far?
- How do you compare to the people around you?
- How do you compare to your ideal?
- What is one of your "that's not who I am" statements?
- What beliefs about yourself do your habits reinforce?

### 🧠 Thought Creations
- List three positive results you have in your life right now.
- List the three thoughts that caused those results.
- List three negative results you have in your life now.
- List the thoughts that caused them.
- List three results you want to create.
- What are the three beliefs you will need to adopt to create those results?

### 😤 Indulgent Feelings
- What is an "indulgent" feeling you would like to stop using?
- What are the thoughts you have that cause this feeling?
- What effect does this feeling have on you?
- What action, reaction or inaction does this feeling cause?
- Do you believe you could go a day, a week, or a month without indulging in this feeling? Why or why not?
- How does this feeling prevent you from achieving what you want in your life?
- Why do you imagine you indulge in this feeling?

### 🌡 Top Three Feelings
- On a daily basis, what are your three most common feelings?
- Why do you feel this way? Explain.
- Describe each feeling and how you currently deal with each one.
- What results do these feelings create in your life?
- What would you like your top three feelings to be?
- Why do you want to feel this way?
- How would your actions be different if you felt this way daily?
- How would your results be different?

### 👤 Emotional Childhood / Adulthood
- Name a person you think causes a negative emotion in you.
- What do they do or say that triggers you?
- What is the emotion you have when this happens?
- Why do you think they have this power over your emotions?
- What is the thought you are having that is really causing your feeling?
- When you blame them for how you feel, what action do you take?
- What are other thought options when they behave this way?

### 📋 Owning Your Life
- What have YOU done to end up where you are? (make a list)
- What did YOU do in your childhood to get here?
- What did YOU do in your young adulthood to get here?
- What did YOU do in your relationships to get the results you have?
- Who do you currently blame in your life? Why?
- What do you currently blame in your life? Why?
- If you owned all the things that have happened in your life, what would that mean for you? Would anything feel different?

### 🎯 Constraint & Focus
- Brainstorm a list of what is important to you in your life. Give yourself five minutes.
- Now prioritize these in order of the top five.
- Why have you chosen these five and put them in this order?
- How much of your time yesterday was spent on each of these priorities?
- What things would you like to eliminate from your life to have more focus?
- How can you make your priorities more constrained, simplified and specific?
- For each priority: how do you have to think, feel and act to get the result you want?

### 🔬 Constrained Focus (90-Day)
- What is one area of focus you would be willing to constrain your attention to for the next 90 days?
- Have you made it as specific and measurable as possible?
- What would you have to eliminate from your mind and your schedule?
- If you accomplish this one thing, does it eliminate other todos you think about?
- How will you know in two weeks if you are on your way?
- What is the exact result you will create?
- What is the plan to make it inevitable?

### ⏰ Decisions Ahead of Time
- Name one area in your life where you make poor decisions in the moment.
- What are the decisions you have currently been making?
- What are the results of these decisions?
- With forethought, what are the decisions you would prefer to make?
- If your present self makes these decisions, how can you ensure your future self follows through?
- What will your future self need to remember to follow through?
- How do you imagine you would be different if you made and honored decisions ahead of time?

### 😱 The Worst Feeling
- The worst feeling I can imagine is...
- Describe this feeling and why you name it as the worst.
- What exactly does the feeling feel like in your body? Where do you feel it?
- What do you imagine would cause a feeling like this?
- How might you react? How might you avoid it? How might you dull it? How might you resist it?
- What is the worst possible outcome of this feeling?

### 📖 The Manual
- Think about someone you want to change and write their name.
- Write, in detail, what you would like them to do.
- For each item, write down why you want them to behave this way.
- How do you think you would feel if they behaved this way?
- How would your thoughts about them change if they behaved this way?
- Do you want them to behave this way even if they don't want to? Why or why not?
- What do you make it mean that they don't behave this way?
- When someone wants you to behave in a certain way so they can feel good, what is that like for you?

### 🚧 Boundaries
- What is the boundary violation?
- Who violated the boundary?
- What is the boundary you want to set? (Request + Self Action)
- What are your fears in setting this boundary?
- What are the benefits of setting this boundary?
- How will you set this boundary?
- How will you ensure you follow through on your self-action?

### ❤️ Unconditional Love
- Think of someone who is challenging for you to love.
- Write the reasons why you find them hard to love.
- What are your thoughts about this person that prevent you from loving them more?
- What conditions have you put on loving them?
- What does it feel like when you have chosen not to love them?
- What would it be like to love them without condition?
- How is unconditional love different than love? How does it serve you?

### 🏆 Outcome Planning
- What is the outcome you want to achieve? Why?
- What are the actions you need to take to achieve it?
- List these items in order and assign a time frame to each.
- What can you accomplish in the next two weeks?
- How could you make this outcome inevitable with massive action?
- How long are you willing to take action to achieve this outcome?

### 🦁 Dancing with Fear (Danser avec la peur)
- Mobilize your VISION. What fears are blocking your path toward your VISION?
- Where do you see the avoidance strategy at work? How are you avoiding situations where fear could manifest?
- Where do you see the minimization strategy at work? How do you choose to ignore the fear or tell yourself it's ridiculous?
- Where do you see the "flight forward" strategy? Where are you trying to outrun the fear through perfectionism?
- What do you think you're protecting yourself from by doing this?
- What is the cost?
- What would be the benefit if you stopped doing this?
- What would it look like if you learned to dance with the fear?
- What would change if you approached this situation by choosing to dance with the fear? What would it make possible?
- What feeling (E) do you need in order to dance with the fear?
- What thought (P) would create that feeling for you?

### 🔑 The 4 Keys to Daring (Les 4 Clés pour OSER)
- Think of a subject, project, or area of your life where you want more, better, or something new. Describe the VISION.
- What is standing in the way of moving toward this goal? Write everything you think.
- What thought or emotion strikes you the most? Complete the model (C-P-E-A-R).
- What beliefs limit you the most in your capacity to dare? Why?
- What is the impact you want to have on the world or on others?
- What are your inner strengths, skills, talents that you can leverage toward your VISION?
- What are your weak points where you'll need external resources or help?
- When you focus on your VISION, what would be the first step to reach it?
- What is the scope of the risk you're taking? What is your plan B?
- Think about who you'll be once this action is accomplished. What thought and emotion (the audacity!) will be most useful?

### 💪 Confidence (SOSI)
- Make a list of every single accomplishment you've ever had.
- What is your fear? What is the worst case scenario? What desired action are you avoiding?
- Create your confident alter ego: What is her self-opinion? How does she handle fear? How does she show up? What does she think of other people's opinions? Give her a name.
- What area of your life do you feel the most confident? What are your top 3 beliefs there?
- What area do you feel the least confident? What are your top 3 beliefs there?

### 💃 Romance Yourself (SOSI)
- Imagine how you want to be loved and romanced by your most amazing lover. Describe the date, the surprise, what they say, how they make you feel. Your best lover is staring back at you in the mirror.
- Spend 30 minutes alone with yourself — no TV, music, or external stimulation. What do you notice? What do you feel? What are you thinking?
- Pick one thing you hold against yourself. From a place of love, ask: why did I do it? What do I need to do to let this go?
- Journal a crazy fantasy. What feeling does it give you? How can you create that feeling today?
- List 25 of your favorite simple pleasures. Do you treat yourself to them? Commit to 5 this month.
- Take yourself on a date. Ask: What do I want for myself?

### ✏️ Thought Editing (SOSI)
- Create your Word Style Guide: What is the tone of your desired thinking? How do you address yourself? What words do you never use? What words do you use often? What are your "instead of" statements?
- Pick one thought holding you back. Build a thought ladder: Current Belief → "I keep thinking..." → Let Go → Open Up → Neutral Thought → Desired Belief. Practice one rung this month.

### 🗣 Speak from Your Future (SOSI)
- Imagine the version of yourself who's reached your extraordinary goal. What phrases are no longer part of her vocabulary?
- What did she stop saying about herself to others?
- What does she refuse to talk about?
- She's at dinner with friends. What is she talking about?
- How does she talk about her future? About money? About love? About her career? About self-care?

### 🧹 Declutter Your Mind (SOSI)
- Complete a Thought Dump (write everything in your head).
- Run each thought through: Does this thought serve me? Would I choose it on purpose? Does it belong in my future?
- List 100 things cluttering your mind and life that you will either: remove, repair, or resolve.

### 🏠 Your Space and Self-Image (SOSI)
- Walk through your home. Describe the favorite part. What 3 adjectives describe it? What does it reflect about you?
- Describe the least favorite part. What 3 adjectives describe it? What does it reflect about you?
- How do you want to feel in this space?
- EDIT: Remove one item that doesn't create this feeling. ADD: Bring in one item that embodies it. APPRECIATE: What is one thing you appreciate?

### 📊 Raise Your Standards (SOSI)
- What are you currently tolerating in your life?
- What do you expect from yourself?
- What standards does your future self hold?
- What is one standard you'll practice raising over the next month?
- Write your Personal Manifesto: "I am a woman who..."

### ⏰ Calendar & Time (SOSI)
- On a scale from 1-10, where would you rate yourself in regards to time? Why?
- What thoughts do you have about time that aren't serving you?
- What thoughts could you choose that will serve you?
- What values does your calendar reflect? What does it say about who you are?
- What is your calendar showing you're avoiding? Why?
- What values do you want your calendar to reflect?

### 🪞 Body Image (SOSI)
- What experiences or opportunities has a poor body image caused you to miss?
- Stand in front of the mirror for 2 minutes. What thoughts do you have? What emotions?
- What 3 changes would you like to see? Why? What do you believe you'll think once you've experienced them?
- Describe the facts about your favorite and least favorite body parts. Then describe the fiction (thoughts).
- Describe in detail what an ideal relationship with your body looks like. How do you treat her?
- Write a one-page love letter to your body.

### 🎯 Self-Image Word of the Year (SOSI)
- Go to the end of your year. Make a list of what you've accomplished.
- What is your one extraordinary goal?
- Who do you need to become to achieve it?
- Choose ONE word that best expresses the energy you're ready to embody.
- Why is this the perfect word for you this year?
- How would your days be different if you embodied this word?
- What would you be able to achieve if you embodied it for one full year?
- What are the 3 main obstacles you anticipate? How will you address each?
- Describe a "day in the life" of the version of you embodying your word.

### 🔄 Self-Image Habits (SOSI)
- List your daily habits. List your weekly habits. Circle those having a negative impact.
- What beliefs about yourself do these habits reinforce?
- Which habit is holding you back the most?
- Watch yourself for 48 hours. List the thoughts that precede this habit.
- Imagine your future self who achieved your goal. Which habits did she stop? What are her habits?
- Choose one new habit. How do you need to see yourself (identity) to have this habit?
- Write your "when this, then that" plan. Set up surroundings. Decide on rewards.

### 🎨 Emotional Mastery (SOSI)
- What emotion are you feeling right now? Describe it in detail.
- Why are you feeling this way? (Look for thoughts, not outside events)
- What does this emotion urge you to do or not do?
- What is your overall state of being? (Top 3 most frequent emotions)
- What would you like your overall state of being to be? (Top 3 desired emotions)
- Imagine that this is who you are. How would you show up differently? What results would you create?
- List 5 things you want. For each: "When I have [this], I'll feel [X]." Choose one feeling and practice it now.
- What is your extraordinary goal? What actions do you need to take? What emotion do you need to create to show up for those actions?

### ⚡ Energy Assessment (SOSI)
- Complete the Energy Assessment across: Relationships, Wellness, Work, Environment, Money.
- Count checked items × 2 = % energy spent on drains.
- List 5 energy drains you will address this month. What is your plan for each?

### 💎 Enjoying Luxury (SOSI)
- What's your luxury story? Your experiences with and thoughts about luxury?
- Collect images that embody what luxury looks and feels like to you.
- Luxury feels like / looks like / tastes like / sounds like / smells like...
- If luxury were a feeling, what would it be? How can you create more of it now?
- What luxuries are available to you right now?
- What luxuries feel out of reach? What would need to change?
- How can you turn daily activities into a more luxurious experience?

### 🏗 Work Habits (SOSI)
- What are your general thoughts about work? How do these make you feel?
- How do these emotions impact your work results?
- Which emotions do you want to feel in your work life? Which thoughts will create them?
- What type of environments give you energy?
- How can you dress for your results?
- Run your task list through the 3-D filter: Delete, Delegate, or Delight.

### 💼 Business & Entrepreneurship (Jody Moore)
- What is the specific problem you will solve? Who will you solve it for? Who is it NOT for?
- Is it a problem this group is willing to pay to solve? Why?
- What is different about your offer from what others offer?
- Why do you believe this is a great offer to build your business around?

### 🌟 The Art of Discipline (Tonya Leigh)
- What feelings do you experience when you hear the word discipline? What thoughts create them?
- What areas of your life do you already practice discipline? What is the result?
- What areas do you NOT practice discipline? What is the result?
- How would you rather feel about discipline?
- What do you want to create? Why? How will you know when you've created it?
- Think about your desire already done. How do you feel? What do you think? How do you show up differently?
- What daily habits do you want to practice? What loving rules do you want in place?
- What one tiny habit will you begin? What will the compound effect be over 6 months?

### 🏰 The Royal Treatment (Tonya Leigh)
- Day 1 THINK: What's my #1 excuse preventing self-care? What's the result of not prioritizing it? If I saw myself as a Queen, what would I think about self-care?
- Day 2 SPEAK: What's the general tone of my self-talk? What one statement about myself is holding me back? If I saw myself as a Queen, how would I speak to myself?
- Day 3 EAT: If I saw myself as a Queen, how would I eat differently?
- Day 4 MOVE: What do I think and feel about exercising? What feeling do I want most from movement? What would my exercise routine look like as a Queen?
- Day 5 DELIGHT: My personal definition of pleasure is... What gets in the way? How would I delight in myself and my life as a Queen?

### 📰 Live Like an Editor (Tonya Leigh)
- What feelings do you want more of? What words do you want people to use to describe you?
- Name a BIG goal. What emotion will you feel once achieved?
- Choose ONE word that best expresses the energy you're ready to embody. This is your title.
- Write headlines about your future self. Write headlines that reframe your past struggles.
- Close your eyes and imagine the most luxurious setting. What do you see? Where do you feel most alive?
- Your surroundings: What items do you notice? Bring ONE element into your environment today.
- Your style: What statement do you want your style to make about you? What in your wardrobe doesn't express it?
- Your thoughts: What is the tone of your desired thinking? "Instead of [X], we say [Y]."

## PART 2: CORE PRINCIPLES REFERENCE

### The CTFAR Model
C (Circumstance) → T (Thought) → F (Feeling) → A (Action) → R (Result)
- C: Neutral, provable fact — what a camera would record
- T: The sentence in the mind — always optional
- F: ONE word — the vibration in the body
- A: What you do, don't do, how you show up (includes inaction and self-talk)
- R: The outcome — always proves T true (self-fulfilling cycle)

### Three Ineffective Fear Strategies (Clotilde Dusoulier)
1. **L'évitement** (avoidance) — avoiding situations where fear could manifest
2. **La minimisation** — dismissing the fear as ridiculous, not giving it attention
3. **La fuite en avant** (flight forward) — trying to outrun fear through perfectionism and hustle

The effective strategy: **Danser avec la peur** — stop avoiding, minimizing, or outrunning. Turn toward the fear, question it, use it as information, explore the worst-case scenario concretely, then ask: what would I actually do?

### The 4 Keys to OSER (Clotilde Dusoulier)
1. Identify the right moment (external triggers: circumstances; internal triggers: emotions as GPS)
2. Know yourself deeply (become your own best ally, identify strengths and weaknesses)
3. Build the path step by step (you don't need to see the whole staircase — just the first step; decide from your future self)
4. Think beyond yourself (shift from ego-fears to a service mindset: what impact do you want to have? What would the world miss if you didn't dare?)

### The Be-Do-Have Model (Tonya Leigh)
Wrong: DO → HAVE → BE ("When I do the diet and have the body, I'll be confident")
Right: BE → DO → HAVE ("When I'm being confident, I'll do confident things and have confident results")
Your identity determines your actions. Change who you're being first.

### Emotions as GPS (Clotilde Dusoulier)
Emotions deliver the most precise information about where you are and where you want to go. But unlike a car GPS, the emotional GPS needs to be decoded. The tipping point for daring is often the intersection between painful emotions about the present (frustration, boredom, resentment) and pleasant emotions about the future.

### Palette of 1000 Emotions (Change ma vie)
11 categories: Tranquillité, Surprise, Stupeur, Joie, Tristesse, Dégoût, Colère, Fureur, Peur, Terreur, Coupure avec ses besoins. Use for precise emotional vocabulary in both French and English.

### Business-Minded Framework (Jody Moore)
1. Set a 30-60 day goal
2. Take an educated guess
3. Output at B- level
4. Invest time or money
5. Observe and learn
6. Make it better

## PART 3: KEY COACHING DISTINCTIONS

- **Thought Ladder**: Current Belief → "I keep thinking..." → Let Go → Open Up → Neutral → Desired Belief
- **Cognitive Dissonance**: The discomfort between old and new identity. Gradual thought edits lessen it.
- **Passive vs Purposeful Thinking**: Passive = accepting thoughts from the past. Purposeful = choosing thoughts based on the future.
- **Emotional Middleman**: The belief that external things create feelings. Cut out the middleman — create feelings directly.
- **Emotional Setpoint**: Like a thermostat. Your mind works to keep you within your current emotional range.
- **Self-Image Capacity**: You can't outperform your self-concept. Raise the thermostat.
- **Enclothed Cognition**: What you wear informs how you feel and act.
- **The 3 D's**: Delete (not effective), Delegate (someone else), Delight (choose to enjoy what remains).
- **When This, Then That**: Link desired behaviors to triggers. Also works for replacement habits.
- **Environment Design**: Set up surroundings to support desired behavior instead of relying on willpower.
- **Leisurely Hustling**: Combine ambition with ease. Schedule leisure FIRST, then build work around it.
